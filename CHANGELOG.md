# Changelog

All notable changes to FunnelRadar are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] — Unreleased

### Added
- Menu bar panel and a window with every project side by side, and one project in detail.
- Funnel leaks, D1/D7/D30 retention with a cohort heatmap, DAU/MAU stickiness, biggest movers,
  unusual days, a weekday pattern and a trend line.
- Alerts with Mac notifications and your own thresholds.
- Setup that installs a pinned `mp`, signs in and picks your apps before the app opens.
- A query budget that stays well inside Mixpanel's limit.
- A local hub for Remote Code.
- Ten languages.
