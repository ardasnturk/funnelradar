## FunnelRadar 0.1.0

The first release. FunnelRadar puts your Mixpanel projects side by side, in your menu bar.

* See where people drop out of your funnels, and how conversion compares with the period before.
* Follow D1, D7 and D30 retention, stickiness, unusual days and your biggest movers.
* Get alerts when conversion or retention falls, or a day looks unusual.

FunnelRadar is free, open source and read-only. It needs macOS 15 and a Mixpanel account, and installs the rest itself (about 300 MB). It stays well inside Mixpanel's limit of 60 queries an hour. It isn't affiliated with Mixpanel.
